# Asset descriptions

Real screenshots of Viste SMS (Next.js web app, same UI ships in the Windows desktop app and Android app), captured in demo mode with sample school data. All screens are 2497x1404 (16:9-ish, light theme), sidebar on the left, white cards on a #f7f9fb canvas.

- `capture/assets/logo.png` — 500x500 school crest: "Viste Senior Academy" lightbulb with graduation cap over an open book, navy and gold. Brand mark for intro and CTA.
- `capture/assets/screen-splash.png` — app loading screen: the crest centered in a white circle with a teal progress ring and "Welcome please wait ..." on a pale canvas. Good opener / app launch moment.
- `capture/assets/screen-dashboard.png` — admin dashboard: headline stat cards (486 students, 32 teachers, 94% attendance, fees collected/outstanding), charts for enrolment, attendance and fee collection, recent activity feed. The "whole school at a glance" hero.
- `capture/assets/screen-students.png` — Students directory: search + filter bar (classes, levels, statuses, genders, subjects, sports), table of 24 students with admission numbers (VHS-2026-xxx), class/level, subjects and Active badges, "Register student" button.
- `capture/assets/screen-fees.png` — Fees & Payments: four stat cards (Total billed $8,380, Collected $6,547.50, Outstanding $1,832.50, Overdue $0), Fee structure card with ECD / Primary / Secondary / A-Level / Non-formal, Day Scholar and Boarder, termly and monthly prices, then Student accounts with Owing / Fees cleared badges and Pay / Passcode buttons.
- `capture/assets/screen-payroll.png` — Payroll for September 2026 (Locked): stat cards (7 employees, gross $5,560, net $5,560), PDF/Excel export, staff table with basic, allowances, gross, net salary and Payslip buttons.
- `capture/assets/screen-finance.png` — Financial dashboard: KPI cards (total revenue $52,670, expenses $20,427, salary $16,680, net balance $32,242, outstanding fees $18,450), Monthly revenue bar chart (teal), Monthly expenses (navy/amber), Revenue vs expenses line chart, Salary trends area chart.
- `capture/assets/fonts/` — Fraunces (variable weight, display serif) and Manrope 400–800 (body sans) woff2 files.
