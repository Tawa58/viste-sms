Viste SMS — Viste High School Management System
Your school. Connected. Simplified.
Manage your school operations, academics, students, attendance, fees and results — all in one place.

Modules (from the app navigation):
- Dashboard: enrolment, attendance today, fees collected, recent activity
- Daily check-in: staff check-in and staff attendance tracking
- People: Students, Teachers & Staff, Parents/Guardians
- Academics: Classes, Subjects, Sports, Clubs & Activities, Attendance registers, Examinations, Results
- Finance: Financial dashboard, Fees & Payments (ECD, Primary, Secondary, A-Level fees for day scholars and boarders, termly or monthly plans, receipts), Expenses, Revenue
- HR & Payroll: staff records, salary scales, payroll runs, deductions, payslips (PDF / Excel)
- Student portal: students sign in with their student number and portal code to see results by term, month or year, download results sheets, see fees
- Parents see attendance, results and fee balances
- Available on the web, as a Windows desktop app and as an Android app
- Website: viste-sms.vercel.app
