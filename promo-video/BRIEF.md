## Intent

A short marketing introduction to Viste SMS (Viste High School Management System) that also
shows how it works: one connected app for the office, teachers, parents and students. The user
asked for "a short video about introduction of this app and how it works and just a marketing".
Confident, modern, trustworthy; for school decision-makers.

## Assets

- capture/assets/*.png — real screenshots of the app taken from its built-in demo data
  (dashboard, students, attendance, results, fees, payroll, student portal, mobile), featured
  in device frames.
- public/viste-logo.png (copied to capture/assets/logo.png) — the school logo, for the open and close.

## Customizations

- Real app screens in animated device frames (user choice).
- Audio: the user will supply their own music track and/or voiceover; build silent with bold
  on-screen text, leave room for an `<audio>` track to be dropped in.
